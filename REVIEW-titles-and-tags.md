## A note on what I filled in and what I did not

**The titles below are my draft, and every one of them needs checking.** I wrote them from the text as I know it, not from the recordings — I have no way to listen to an audio file. For the first 37 my confidence is reasonably good; from 38 onward it drops enough that I left them blank rather than put words in the tradition's mouth. Please treat all of them as a starting point, not an answer. Anyone who knows the verses will spot a wrong opening line instantly, which is exactly why they are safe to draft.

**The theme tags are blank on purpose, and this is a change from what I said I would do.** I had planned to draft those too. Having thought about it, I shouldn't. The *phalashruti* — which verse is chanted for which benefit — varies genuinely and substantially between lineages and printed editions. And unlike a title, a wrong tag is invisible: it looks exactly as plausible as a right one. A draft would take longer to check than to write fresh, and a mistake could slip through into someone's practice. This is Swetha's tradition and her call. If she has a list already, use it as-is.

Adding a new theme needs no help from me. Type a new word after the second `|` on any line — `progeny`, `debt`, `speech` — and a tab for it appears on the site by itself.

**One thing not to do:** don't tag the dhyanam or Slokam 01 to make them show up at the top of each theme. The site already puts them there, automatically, at the head of every playlist. Tag them only if they genuinely belong to that theme on their own merits.
